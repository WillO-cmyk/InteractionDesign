About the Artist

John Ritter

John Ritter is a designer known for his font, Rad, which he created for Adobe Fonts and MyFonts. He adapted Rad from a skateboard alphabet he made for a college assignment and worked at Adobe before opening his own studio. His typography is characterized by its unique, illustrative style.

Designer: John Ritter Most Famous Font: Rad Origin: Skateboard alphabet (college assignment) Context: Rad captures the "no rules" skateboard spirit Availability: Adobe Fonts, MyFonts Background: Ex-Adobe; founder of Ritter Illustration
