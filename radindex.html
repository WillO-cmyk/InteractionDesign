John Ritter is a designer known for his font, Rad, which he created for Adobe Fonts and MyFonts. He adapted Rad from a skateboard alphabet he made for a college assignment and worked at Adobe before opening his own studio. Designer John Ritter created the font . He adapted Rad from a skateboard alphabet that he created for a college design assignment. In Rad, he captures the "no rules" spirit of the skateboard scene-one John was rolled up in when he wasn't busy studying. Rad is part of the Linotype Library.
